# Third-party rule sources

`FillerPhrases.yml`, `HedgingPhrases.yml`, `ContrastiveFormulas.yml`, `OpeningCliches.yml`,
`AICompoundPhrases.yml`, and `SycophancyMarkers.yml` are adapted from
[tbhb/vale-ai-tells](https://github.com/tbhb/vale-ai-tells) (MIT License, Copyright (c)
2025-2026 Tony Burns). `SycophancyMarkers.yml` was pruned of bare-word tokens (`Absolutely`,
`Certainly`, `Of course`) that false-positive on ordinary usage; `FillerPhrases.yml` had
`at the end of the day` appended.

`Vocabulary.yml` is independently written, not derived from any third-party style package.
